# Voting Age Eligibility Checker

## What it does
Enter your age and it tells you whether you are eligible to vote.
If not eligible it tells you exactly how many years are left.

## What I learned
- Storing the voting age as a named constant
- Calculating years remaining dynamically
- Singular vs plural grammar for years remaining
- Separating eligibility logic from display logic

## How to run
Open index.html in your browser.


**Test it with these values:**
- 18 → Eligible to vote
- 25 → Eligible to vote
- 17 → 1 year to go
- 10 → 8 years to go
- 0 → 18 years to go
- negative → Age cannot be negative
- empty → Please enter a valid age
