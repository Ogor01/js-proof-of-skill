# Leap Year Checker

## What it does
Enter any year and it tells you whether it is a leap year or not.
Uses the correct three rule leap year algorithm.

## What I learned
- The three rules that determine a leap year
- Why the order of conditions matters in the algorithm
- Divisibility checks with the modulo operator

## How to run
Open index.html in your browser.


**Test it with these values:**
- 2024 → Leap Year
- 2000 → Leap Year
- 1900 → Not a Leap Year
- 2023 → Not a Leap Year
- 2100 → Not a Leap Year
- 1600 → Leap Year
